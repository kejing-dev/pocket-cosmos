# Music-box score sources

All imported scores retain the complete selected MIDI part timeline. Simultaneous notes are reduced to the highest pitch at each onset for one-ball playback. Only leading silence is removed. This is a complete **single-line music-box arrangement**, not the original polyphonic orchestration. No note-count or duration cutoff is applied.
